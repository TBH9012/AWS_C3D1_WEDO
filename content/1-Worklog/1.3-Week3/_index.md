### Week 3 Objectives:
* Understand relational database management concepts with Amazon RDS.
* Deploy auto-scaling and load-balanced applications using Auto Scaling & ELB.
* Manage, control, and optimize AWS costs utilizing AWS Budgets.
* Comprehensively monitor systems, resources, and applications via Amazon CloudWatch.

### Week 3 Achievements:
* **Database Management (RDS):** Mastered foundational knowledge of AWS managed relational databases. Understood differences in storage options, when to utilize Multi-AZ for high availability, and optimal use cases for OLTP workloads.
* **Scalable Architecture (Auto Scaling & ELB):** Successfully deployed a highly available, fault-tolerant architecture that automatically scales resources to maintain performance during traffic spikes while optimizing costs during low demand.
* **Cost Control (AWS Budgets):** Mastered proactive AWS cost monitoring. Successfully configured real-time alerts for budget thresholds and grasped the flexibility of using Savings Plans over Reserved Instances for EC2.
* **System Monitoring (CloudWatch):** Achieved comprehensive observability of applications and infrastructure. Perfected skills in setting Alarms to reduce Mean Time To Recovery (MTTR) and created custom Dashboards for real-time system health tracking.
